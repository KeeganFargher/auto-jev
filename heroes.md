I think the key is to make each Jev immediately readable from a distance, with one strong fantasy and a few abilities that can create ridiculous synergies with doctrines and relics.

# Jevs: Initial Hero Roster

## Core concept

Each faction begins a run with **one Jev**.

A Jev is:

- a named mythical hero
- a powerful battlefield unit
- the faction's most recognisable character
- capable of levelling during the run
- able to acquire relics
- able to choose between branching abilities
- permanently killable

They should feel closer to a simplified Dota hero than an RTS commander.

The army still matters. A Jev should not normally solo an entire faction.

But a strong Jev, combined with the right army and a broken roguelike build, should occasionally become absurd.

That is desirable.

---

# How Jev progression works

Keep their progression relatively small.

For example:

**Level 1**
Starting passive + starting ability.

**Level 3**
Choose one of two upgrades.

**Level 5**
Choose one of two new abilities.

**Level 7**
Choose one of two specialisations.

**Level 10**
Choose an Ultimate evolution.

This means the Jev develops differently between runs.

A Storm Jev might become an enormous burst-damage character in one run and a battlefield-control character in another.

Jev chooses every branch based on the current world state.

# 1. Veyra, the Stormborn

### Role

Damage / Control

### Fantasy

A lightning-wielding Jev who becomes increasingly dangerous when enemies cluster together.

Visually:

Dark robes, glowing electric cracks, floating fragments around her hands.

Extremely readable in low-poly 3D.

### Passive: Static

Attacking enemies applies **Static**.

Enemies with Static can conduct lightning to nearby enemies.

This immediately creates synergy with large armies.

### Ability: Chain Bolt

Lightning jumps between nearby enemies.

More Static means more jumps.

### Level 3

**Forked Current**

Chain Bolt gains additional jumps.

or

**Storm Charge**

Moving builds electrical charge. The next attack deals increased damage.

### Level 5

**Thunderstrike**

Calls lightning onto a targeted battlefield area after a short delay.

or

**Storm Field**

Creates an electrical zone that slows enemies and continuously applies Static.

### Level 7

**Overload**

Enemies killed while electrified explode.

or

**Living Conductor**

Nearby friendly soldiers gain lightning damage on their attacks.

### Ultimate

**Tempest**

Veyra creates a huge moving storm around herself.

Lightning automatically strikes enemies inside it.

With the right build, this could become horrifying.

### Roguelike insanity

Imagine:

- War Drums
- attack-speed stacking
- Chain Bolt
- Static explosions
- relic that triggers abilities on kills

A huge enemy army becomes Veyra's best possible target.

---

# 2. Thorn, the Ancient

### Role

Tank / Defence / Nature

### Fantasy

A gigantic nature Jev formed from roots, stone and ancient trees.

Very slow.

Very difficult to kill.

### Passive: Living Bark

Thorn slowly regenerates health while outside combat.

Nearby defensive structures also regenerate slowly.

### Ability: Rootwall

Creates a temporary wall of roots.

It blocks movement and can split enemy armies.

This is potentially extremely strong around chokepoints.

### Level 3

**Ironwood**

Thorn gains armour while standing still.

or

**Wild Growth**

Living Bark also heals nearby soldiers.

### Level 5

**Entangle**

Roots erupt underneath enemies and immobilise them briefly.

or

**Seed Bastion**

Creates a temporary defensive tower made from living wood.

### Level 7

**Ancient Protector**

Damage dealt to nearby structures is partially redirected to Thorn.

or

**Marching Forest**

Nearby soldiers gain armour and regeneration while following Thorn.

### Ultimate

**Awaken the Earth**

Roots erupt across a massive area.

Enemy movement is heavily restricted while friendly structures and units regenerate.

### Roguelike insanity

Combine:

- Citadel
- Repair Crews
- Thorn
- structure regeneration
- defensive relics

and one faction can turn into an absolutely disgusting fortress.

---

# 3. Kael, the Bloodhound

### Role

Melee DPS / Snowball

### Fantasy

An aggressive supernatural warrior who becomes stronger as the battle gets bloodier.

This is the Jev you want to see running directly into an army.

### Passive: Bloodlust

Enemy deaths nearby temporarily increase Kael's attack speed.

Stacks.

### Ability: Hunt

Kael selects a high-value enemy and charges towards them.

He gains movement speed while pursuing them.

### Level 3

**Predator**

Killing the hunted target resets Hunt.

or

**Frenzy**

Bloodlust stacks last significantly longer.

### Level 5

**Cleave**

Attacks damage enemies around Kael.

or

**Execution**

Kael deals massively increased damage to heavily wounded enemies.

### Level 7

**Feast**

Kills restore health.

or

**Pack Leader**

Nearby infantry also gain a portion of Bloodlust.

### Ultimate

**Red Mist**

For a short period:

- enormous movement speed
- enormous attack speed
- cannot retreat

Kills extend the duration.

This should occasionally go completely out of control.

### Roguelike insanity

Kael + Conscription + Blood Price + Red Standard could become the quintessential swarm snowball build.

If he gets the first few kills, the whole army starts accelerating.

If he doesn't, they can get wiped out.

Perfect.

---

# 4. Elowen, the Lifebinder

### Role

Support / Healing

### Fantasy

A mythical healer who manipulates life energy rather than simply casting traditional healing spells.

Visually:

Floating green-white ribbons connecting her to nearby units.

### Passive: Lifeline

Elowen automatically links herself to the most wounded nearby friendly unit.

A portion of damage they receive is redirected to Elowen.

### Ability: Renewal

Heals a group of nearby units over time.

### Level 3

**Shared Burden**

Lifeline can connect to multiple units.

or

**Overflow**

Excess healing becomes temporary armour.

### Level 5

**Rescue**

Pulls a critically wounded friendly unit towards Elowen.

or

**Rebirth Seed**

Places a seed on a friendly unit. If they die shortly afterwards, they return with low health.

### Level 7

**Battlefield Medicine**

Units healed by Elowen temporarily gain attack speed.

or

**Sanctuary**

Elowen can create a small zone where friendly units take reduced damage.

### Ultimate

**Second Breath**

All nearby fallen ordinary soldiers from the previous few seconds return temporarily.

If they survive until the effect ends, they remain alive.

This could create incredible last-second battle reversals.

### Roguelike insanity

Elowen combined with:

- elite veterans
- armour stacking
- regeneration
- Old Guard

could create armies that simply refuse to die.

---

# 5. Nyx, the Wayfinder

### Role

Utility / Mobility / Sabotage

### Fantasy

A strange shadow Jev who manipulates space, vision and movement.

Nyx should make armies behave differently rather than simply making numbers bigger.

### Passive: Veil

Nyx and nearby scouts become harder for enemies to detect while travelling outside claimed territory.

### Ability: Shadowstep

Nyx instantly moves a short distance.

Can cross certain terrain obstacles.

### Level 3

**Long Step**

Shadowstep range increases.

or

**Echo**

Shadowstep leaves behind a decoy.

### Level 5

**Waygate**

Creates two temporary connected portals.

Units can move between them.

or

**Blackout**

Temporarily removes enemy vision in an area.

### Level 7

**Saboteur**

Nyx can disable an enemy structure temporarily.

or

**Pathfinder**

Armies travelling with Nyx move considerably faster.

### Ultimate

**Fold the World**

Nyx can temporarily teleport an entire squad to another visible location.

This could create some incredible AI decisions:

> Enemy army attacking our base.

> Teleport entire force behind their base.

Now suddenly you're watching a base race.

### Roguelike insanity

Nyx does not necessarily become statistically overpowered.

Instead, she can break the normal geometry of the game.

That is another kind of broken.

---

# 6. Orun, the Forgefather

### Role

Support / Siege / Army Enhancement

### Fantasy

A mythical blacksmith carrying an enormous hammer and dragging a small magical forge with him.

Orun makes the army around him better.

### Passive: Masterwork

Nearby military units slowly improve their equipment while outside combat.

Veterans benefit more.

### Ability: Temper

Temporarily massively increases one squad's armour and damage.

### Level 3

**Fine Steel**

Masterwork improves weapons more quickly.

or

**Heavy Plate**

Masterwork focuses more strongly on defence.

### Level 5

**Warforge**

Orun constructs a temporary forward forge.

Nearby squads can reinforce and repair equipment there.

or

**Shatter**

A massive hammer strike damages structures and removes some armour.

### Level 7

**Master Armourer**

Veteran units receive additional equipment bonuses.

or

**Siege Master**

Nearby siege units gain range and reload speed.

### Ultimate

**Living Arsenal**

For a limited period, every nearby friendly soldier receives upgraded magical equipment.

Ordinary infantry temporarily fight like elite troops.

### Roguelike insanity

Orun + Veteran Command + elite equipment + Old Guard could create a tiny army of absolutely monstrous veterans.

Rather than:

> 40 cheap soldiers

you could have:

> 7 terrifying old bastards who have survived the entire run.

# Initial roster shape

The six create a useful spread:

| Jev    | Primary identity | What they change                           |
| ------ | ---------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| Veyra  | Damage / Control | Punishes grouped armies                    |
| Thorn  | Defence / Tank   | Changes territory and fortifications       |
| Kael   | DPS / Snowball   | Turns kills into momentum                  |
| Elowen | Support          | Makes armies extremely difficult to finish |
| Nyx    | Utility          | Breaks movement and positioning rules      |
| Orun   | Support / Siege  | Creates elite armies and siege builds      |

The important distinction is that they do not simply have different damage numbers.

They alter **how that faction plays the map**.

---

# Jevs and roguelike builds

The coolest part should be that the Jev is only one ingredient.

Consider Veyra.

In one run she gets:

**Chain Bolt → Forked Current → Overload**

plus ranged troops and attack-speed relics.

She becomes a damage monster.

In another:

**Storm Field → Living Conductor**

plus defensive troops.

Now she sits behind the army and turns everyone into lightning units.

Same Jev.

Completely different run.

# Jev Drafting

I would not necessarily let every faction select from all six immediately.

At the start of each run, perhaps every faction receives:

> **Choose one of three Jevs.**

Jev chooses.

Example:

## Thornwatch

Available:

**Kael**
Aggressive melee snowball

**Nyx**
Mobility and sabotage

**Orun**
Elite army and siege

Jev evaluates:

- starting geography
- faction modifier
- world modifier
- nearby resources

and selects one.

This gives you another really enjoyable early spectator moment.

---

# Duplicate Jevs

Initially:

**No duplicate Jevs in the same game.**

If Thornwatch selects Kael, nobody else can.

That creates clearer visual identity.

You immediately recognise:

> Lightning faction.

> Nature faction.

> Kael faction.

Later you could experiment with duplicates if there are skins, mutations or variant builds.

---

# Jev death

Jev death should matter enormously.

But I would not automatically eliminate the civilisation.

Instead:

- large morale penalty
- Jev relic drops
- killer receives a reward
- faction loses Jev abilities
- event appears prominently
- potentially unlock succession or revenge mechanics later

This creates huge moments without ending every match because someone got caught badly.

The dropped relic is particularly interesting.

Imagine Kael has become ridiculously powerful.

Everyone wants him dead.

Eventually Greyhaven manages to kill him.

Kael drops:

## Heart of the Bloodhound

> Kills increase movement speed.

Now Greyhaven gets to incorporate part of Kael's build into theirs.

That is extremely roguelike.

---

# Jev versus Jev battles

These should be rare enough that they're exciting.

When two Jevs enter the same battle, the Director Camera should immediately care.

Something like:

> **VEYRA AND KAEL HAVE ENTERED BATTLE**

Not necessarily huge UI everywhere, but enough that the spectator knows:

this might matter.

Because they have abilities rather than simply bigger health pools, those fights could be very unpredictable.

Kael needs to get onto Veyra.

Veyra needs to keep soldiers between herself and Kael.

Thorn can physically trap Kael.

Nyx can reposition around Thorn.

Elowen can keep Kael alive.

Orun can turn ordinary soldiers into enough of a threat that Kael cannot simply dive the backline.

You start getting actual composition interactions.

---

# The important limitation

I would resist giving every Jev six active abilities.

Remember that nobody is manually controlling them.

Start with:

**1 passive**

**1 basic signature ability**

**1 additional ability gained through levelling**

**1 ultimate**

and then let the skill tree mostly **modify those abilities**.

That is enough.

Otherwise Jev is constantly deciding between eight spells instead of making the more interesting strategic decisions.

The game should handle obvious ability usage automatically.

For example:

> Kael is surrounded by nine enemies.

Code can know Cleave is useful.

Jev does not need to classify every sword swing.

But when Kael's ultimate becomes available before a major assault, _that_ could be a tactical Jev decision:

> Use Red Mist now?

> Save it?

That is consequential enough to ask.

---

# The overall principle

The Jev provides the faction's **fantasy**.

The doctrine system provides the faction's **build**.

The world provides the faction's **problems**.

Jev determines how those three collide.

That gives you runs like:

> "Kael got Conscription and Blood Price and completely rolled everyone."

or:

> "Thorn got Citadel and Greyhaven became impossible to kill."

or:

> "Nyx somehow teleported past the entire army and destroyed their base."

or:

> "Elowen kept seven veterans alive through a battle they absolutely should have lost."

Those are exactly the kinds of stories I think this game should be generating.

I’d probably do the **visual identities and personalities next**, then decide how much of each Jev’s behaviour comes from their built-in nature versus the faction’s Jev decisions.
