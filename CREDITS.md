# Credits

Third-party art in this repo. The raw packs sit in `art/vendor/<pack>/`
(Git LFS) next to their licence files. `pnpm models:import` writes the
trimmed files the game loads to `apps/client/public/assets/models/`.

Sounds and music are made for this game (see `docs/audio.md`), so they
need no credit here.
